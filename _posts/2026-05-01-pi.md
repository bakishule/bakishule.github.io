Since completing my PhD in 2020, I have been reflecting on the journey that has followed. What started as a transition into independent research has grown into an opportunity to lead and contribute to impactful work. Over the past few years, I have been able to secure and lead seven research projects as a Principal Investigator .

<img src="/assets/images/research1.JPG" alt="">

These projects span across AI, data science and agriculture, with a strong focus on solving real-world challenges:
1. Geo-AI and citizen science-powered soybean rust disease surveillance and forecasting

2. Scaling kilimoAI for Early Diagnosis of Maize and Common Bean Diseases in Northern Tanzania

3. Machine Learning Tools for Early Detection of Maize and Common Bean Diseases for Climate Change Adaptation in Tanzania

4. Deep Learning Tools for Early Detection of Diseases Affecting Common Bean and Irish Potato in the Southern Highlands Regions of Tanzania

5. Data Science Africa Research Assistant Program

6. Deep Learning Techniques for Early Detection of Crop Diseases

7. Lacuna Fund Project on Developing Machine Learning Datasets for Crop Diseases: Imagery and Spectrometry Data

<img src="/assets/images/research2.JPG" alt="">

Each project has contributed to my growth, not just as a researcher, but as a leader. Moving into the role of Principal Investigator meant learning how to manage teams, build collaborations across institutions, and align research with real community needs. It also required balancing technical depth with practical impact, ensuring that solutions go beyond theory and reach the people they are meant to serve.

<img src="/assets/images/research3.JPG" alt="">

One of the most meaningful aspects of this journey has been working at the intersection of AI and agriculture. From developing early disease detection systems to building datasets and digital tools, the goal has always been: use technology to improve livelihoods, strengthen food security, and support data-driven decision-making.

<img src="/assets/images/research4.JPG" alt="">

Looking back, this journey has been one of continuous learning, collaboration and growth. Looking ahead, I remain committed to advancing research that is not only innovative, but also inclusive, practical and impactful.
