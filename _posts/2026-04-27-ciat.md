Early this week, I had the opportunity to visit the field and explore practical applications of AI and digital tools in agriculture.

<img src="/assets/images/field1.jpg" alt="">

This visit followed an invitation from the Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT, our new project collaborator. It was a great chance to see firsthand the innovative technologies they are developing and how they are applied in real farming environments.

<img src="/assets/images/field2.jpg" alt="">

During the visit, their team showcased different tools in action, demonstrating how digital solutions can support accurate data collection and improve decision-making in agriculture.

<img src="/assets/images/field3.jpg" alt="">

We also had dedicated time to plan for the implementation of our new project. These discussions were important in aligning our approaches and identifying how we can work together to create meaningful impact for farmers.

<img src="/assets/images/field4.jpg" alt="">

This experience reinforced the value of collaboration in building solutions that are practical, scalable, and responsive to real-world needs. I look forward to the journey ahead as we bring this project to life together.
