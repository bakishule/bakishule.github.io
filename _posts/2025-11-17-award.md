I’m humbled and grateful to be recognized by the Society for AI as one of Africa’s 40 Under-40 AI Achievers.

<img src="/assets/images/award1.jpg" alt="">

This recognition is both an honor and a reminder of the responsibility that comes with working in the field of artificial intelligence. It reflects not just individual effort, but the support, collaboration, and shared vision of many people working to advance AI across the continent.

What motivates me most is the opportunity to contribute to building AI systems that are meaningful, inclusive, and responsible. There is so much potential for AI to address real challenges and improve lives, and it’s important that we shape it in ways that truly serve our communities.

I’m encouraged to keep pushing forward, learning, and contributing to a future where AI plays a positive and impactful role across Africa.
