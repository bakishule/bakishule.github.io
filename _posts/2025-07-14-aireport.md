I had a great time collaborating with AI Community Tanzania to develop the AI Readiness Assessment Report, funded by UNESCO. This project brought together ideas, expertise, and a shared vision for the future of AI in our country.

<img src="/assets/images/Ram1.jpg" alt="">

Working on this report was a valuable experience. It gave us the chance to assess where we stand as a country in terms of AI adoption, policy, and innovation, while also identifying areas that need more attention.

<img src="/assets/images/Ram2.jpg" alt="">

One thing stood out clearly: building inclusive and responsible AI systems requires collaboration across sectors. From researchers to policymakers, everyone has a role to play in shaping how AI benefits society.

This report marks an important step forward for Tanzania as we continue to explore the potential of AI in a way that is ethical, inclusive, and impactful.
📄 Read the full report here: https://tanzania.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-07/National%20AI%20Readiness%20Report.pdf
