Good news to share! Our project on kilimoAI mobile app for crop disease detection has been featured in Nature.

<img src="/assets/images/nature.JPG" alt="">

This recognition highlights the potential of AI in agriculture, especially in supporting farmers to detect crop diseases early and take timely action. It’s encouraging to see work like this gaining visibility on a global platform.


The project focuses on using AI to make disease detection more accessible and practical, particularly for communities where resources and expert support may be limited. It’s a step toward improving productivity, reducing losses, and strengthening food security.


Grateful to everyone who contributed to this work and to the opportunity to keep building solutions that make a real difference.
