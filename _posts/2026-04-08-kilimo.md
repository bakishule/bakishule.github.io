We are excited to receive a Phase 2 grant from Grow Further to scale kilimoAI.

<img src="/assets/images/kilimo1.JPG" alt="">

This support comes at an important time as we work to expand deployment across more districts in Tanzania. Our goal is to reach more smallholder farmers with timely and accessible crop disease detection, helping them respond early and reduce losses.

kilimoAI is built around the idea that technology should be practical and useful for those who need it most. By combining AI with local agricultural needs, we are working to make farming more resilient and informed.


We’re looking forward to the next phase of this journey, strengthening food security and improving livelihoods through innovation that makes a real difference on the ground.
