On 17 April 2026, I had the opportunity to engage with bright and brilliant girls in Arusha, and it was truly an inspiring experience.

<img src="/assets/images/arusha1.JPG" alt="">

The event, organized by the Universal Communications Service Access Fund, brought together girls from different regions to learn how to use drones. It was exciting to see them explore this technology with such enthusiasm and curiosity.

<img src="/assets/images/arusha2.jpg" alt="">

I was deeply impressed by their passion, their bold dreams, and their dedication to building the future. The energy in the room reflected a generation that is ready to embrace innovation and take part in shaping the digital world.

<img src="/assets/images/arusha3.JPG" alt="">

Experiences like this remind us why it’s important to invest in young talent. When we nurture their skills and give them access to emerging technologies, we open doors for real impact.

Let’s continue to support, inspire, and empower the next generation to lead change.
